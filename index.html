Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I apply with poor credit?

Yes, it's worth applying. Because funding through our partners is often secured against an asset, some applications go through where unsecured credit wouldn't. We'll always be honest with you about where you stand — even when the answer is "not yet."

Can I still use my vehicle?

In most cases, yes. You keep driving and using your vehicle as normal — ownership stays with you throughout, provided you meet your repayment obligations.

Will I pay upfront fees?

No. There are no upfront fees for using CapitalAssist to explore your options, and there's no fee if your application isn't successful. Any fees connected to a specific funding agreement are set out clearly by the funding partner before you sign anything.

How long does it take?

Your Funding Concierge Score takes about 45 seconds. From there, a consultant is typically in touch within one working day, and timelines to a final outcome depend on which funding partner and product is the right fit for you.

What documents do I need?

Typically a South African ID, your driver's licence, proof of address, your latest 3 months' payslips and bank statements, and — where relevant — your vehicle or property documents. We'll tell you exactly what's needed for your specific application.

Is my information safe?

Yes. We handle your personal information in line with POPIA, and we only share what's necessary with the funding partners assessing your application.

Am I obligated to accept an offer?

Not at all. Getting your Funding Concierge Score and completing an application costs you nothing and commits you to nothing. You decide if and when you want to go ahead.
